8. Conventions and limits#

This chapter collects the conventions of the library and the limits of the models of the preceding chapters.

8.1. Conventions#

Item

Convention

Coordinates

cylindrical \((x, \theta, r)\), \(x\) along the axis; the element uses \(x\), the arc length \(y = R\theta\) of the mid-surface and \(z = r - R\), Eq. (1.1)

Local basis

\(\boldsymbol{e}_x\), \(\boldsymbol{e}_y\), \(\boldsymbol{e}_z\) right-handed, \(\boldsymbol{e}_z\) the outward normal

Displacements

\(u\) axial, \(v\) circumferential, \(w\) normal, positive outwards

Rotations of the normal

\(\beta_x = -w_{,x}\), \(\beta_y = -w_{,y} + k_v v\), \(k_v = 0\) (Donnell-type) or \(1/R\) (Sanders-type), Eq. (1.4)

Strains

engineering shear strain \(\gamma_{xy}\); curvatures as derivatives of the rotations of the normal, \(\kappa_{xx} = -w_{,xx}\), Eqs. (1.6) and (1.8)

Resultants

\(N_{xx}\), \(N_{yy}\), \(N_{xy}\) positive in tension; \(N_{xx} < 0\) for an axial compression

Node order

counterclockwise in \((x, y)\) from the corner of the smallest \(x\) and \(y\), see The BFSC element on the cylinder

Degrees of freedom of a node

\(u\), \(u_{,x}\), \(u_{,y}\), \(v\), \(v_{,x}\), \(v_{,y}\), \(w\), \(w_{,x}\), \(w_{,y}\), \(w_{,xy}\), at the positions c to c + 9

Pressure

per unit undeformed area, positive along \(+\boldsymbol{e}_z\); an external pressure is negative

Load multiplier

\(\lambda\) multiplies the reference load that produced \(\boldsymbol{K}_G\), or the resultants given to update_KG_constant_stress()

Load stiffness

\(\boldsymbol{K}_F = -\partial \boldsymbol{F}_p/\partial \boldsymbol{u}\), added to the tangent stiffness matrix

Residual

\(\boldsymbol{R} = \boldsymbol{F}_{int} - \lambda \boldsymbol{F}_{ext}\)

Units

any consistent set; the library has no unit conversion

Sparse storage

row, column and value arrays per element, summed on assembly, see Assembling a model

The functions of both kinematics take the same arguments and use the same conventions; bfsccylinder.quadrature.get_points_weights() gives the integration points, in increasing order, used in both directions.

8.2. Limits of the models#

  • Geometry: a perfect circular cylinder of constant radius, meshed with a structured grid of rectangles in \(x\) and \(y\). Cones, doubly curved shells, geometric imperfections and cut-outs that break the structured grid are not modelled.

  • Thin walls: Kirchhoff-Love kinematics without transverse shear deformation and stiffnesses without terms in \(h/R\); the element is not meant for thick or sandwich walls, whose transverse shear flexibility matters.

  • Kinematics: small strains and moderate rotations; the rotation about the normal is not in the nonlinear strains. The Donnell-type kinematics are in addition restricted to deformations with many circumferential waves, see Kinematics of the cylindrical shell.

  • Material: linear elastic laminate, stiffnesses referred to the mid-surface, given at the integration points; the mass matrix assumes a density uniform through the thickness and the reference surface at its middle.

  • Loads: concentrated nodal forces, prescribed displacements, a dead or follower pressure function of the undeformed coordinates, and, for linear buckling, an assumed constant membrane state. A pressure that depends on the current position, e.g. a hydrostatic head, is not modelled.

  • Integration: exact for constant stiffnesses with the numbers of points of Numerical integration; with a varying stiffness or pressure, the integration points sample it.

  • Analyses: the library provides the element matrices and vectors; the assembly, the boundary conditions and the solution of the linear, eigenvalue and nonlinear systems are done by the user, with SciPy or structsolve, see Analyses.
